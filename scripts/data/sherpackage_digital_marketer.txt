DIGITAL MARKETER
Sherpackage | Full-time | Abuja, Nigeria

ABOUT SHERPACKAGE
Sherpackage is a software and technology development company. We are looking for
a Digital Marketer who can turn clear product messaging and well-measured campaigns
into qualified business enquiries, product demonstrations, and customer growth.

THE ROLE
You will plan and execute digital marketing for our software products and development
services. You will work with leadership, product, and sales stakeholders to understand
our customers, explain our solutions, and improve the journey from first visit to
qualified enquiry. This is a hands-on individual-contributor role.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Build an audience-led marketing plan and maintain a monthly content calendar.
- Write clear website, landing-page, email, social, case-study, and campaign copy.
- Manage relevant social channels and engage professionally with our community.
- Improve organic discovery through keyword research, on-page SEO, and useful content.
- Plan and optimise paid campaigns within approved budgets and approval processes.
- Set up and validate conversion events, campaign tags, and lead-source tracking.
- Coordinate lead capture, qualification, nurturing, and handover with sales.
- Test messaging, creative, landing pages, and calls to action; document the results.
- Report weekly on qualified leads, demos, conversion, spend, and campaign learning.
- Protect customer information, use consent-aware outreach, and make accurate claims.

ESSENTIAL REQUIREMENTS
- At least two years of practical digital marketing experience, or comparable
  demonstrable hands-on campaign ownership and results.
- A portfolio showing your contribution, audience, approach, and measured outcomes.
- Strong written English, editing, storytelling, and stakeholder communication.
- Practical knowledge of SEO, content marketing, social publishing, and email campaigns.
- Ability to manage paid campaigns and interpret results without relying on vanity metrics.
- Working knowledge of Google Analytics 4, campaign tagging, conversion tracking,
  spreadsheets, and CRM or lead-management workflows.
- Ability to produce or brief suitable visual assets and coordinate with designers.
- Strong organisation, attention to detail, and ownership of deadlines and budgets.
- Ability to fulfil a full-time role based in Abuja. Working arrangements will be
  clarified during recruitment; a remote or hybrid arrangement is not promised.

EDUCATION
A qualification in marketing, communications, business, or a related discipline is
welcome. Equivalent practical experience and a strong portfolio are equally considered.

NICE TO HAVE
- B2B software, SaaS, ERP, or technology-services marketing experience.
- Experience with LinkedIn campaigns, Google Ads, or Meta Ads Manager.
- Google Search Console, Google Tag Manager, and marketing automation experience.
- Basic design or video-editing skills using tools such as Canva or Figma.
- Experience explaining technical products through case studies and demonstrations.
- Responsible use of AI tools, with fact-checking and human editorial review.

FIRST 90 DAYS
Days 1-30: understand our products and customer segments; audit channels and tracking;
agree lead definitions, baseline metrics, an approved budget, and a prioritised plan.
Days 31-60: launch approved campaigns and content; test landing pages and messaging;
establish lead handover and a regular performance report.
Days 61-90: improve the best-performing activities; report verified outcomes and
recommend the next quarter's priorities based on evidence.

HOW SUCCESS IS MEASURED
Qualified leads, completed product demonstrations, useful published content, reliable
delivery, accurate campaign tracking, and documented experimentation. Specific targets
will be agreed with the manager after reviewing baseline results and available budget.
Follower counts and impressions alone will not define success.

APPLICATION
Apply through the job's application form with your CV and a portfolio link or examples
of two relevant campaigns. Explain your role and the results without sharing confidential
client information. Include your availability or notice period where the form allows.

SELECTION PROCESS
Portfolio review, a structured interview, and a short hypothetical campaign exercise
(approximately 45 minutes). The exercise is for assessment, not unpaid live client work.

Salary is undisclosed and will be discussed during recruitment. No application closing
date has been set. Assessment will focus on job-related skills and evidence of delivery.
